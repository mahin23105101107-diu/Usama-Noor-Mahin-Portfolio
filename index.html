How I approach a network build

Whether designing a multi-department office LAN in Cisco Packet Tracer or provisioning a MikroTik gateway in WinBox, I follow a structured four-stage process.

  1. IP Addressing & Subnet Planning

    Calculate departmental host counts first and allocate clean VLSM / CIDR blocks with room for future growth before touching any CLI.

  2. Logical Topology & VLAN Segmentation

    Separate HR, IT, Sales, and Management broadcast domains on Layer 2 switches and map 802.1Q trunk uplinks to the core router.

  3. Routing, Hardening & Policy Setup

    Configure Router-on-a-Stick sub-interfaces, DHCP pools, NAT/PAT translation, SSH management, port security, and explicit ACL rules.

  4. End-to-End Verification & Documentation

    Confirm reachability and isolation across subnets, then record interface tables and addressing diagrams for repeatable troubleshooting.
