Lab execution steps & additional builds

The ordered sequence used to deploy the Enterprise LAN topology alongside other CCNA and MikroTik labs I practice.

  1. Create VLANs and Assign Access Ports on SW1

    Defined VLANs 10, 20, 30, and 99, bound switchport ranges in access mode, and enabled port security on unused interfaces.

  2. Configure 802.1Q Trunk & Router-on-a-Stick

    Set SW1 Fa0/1 to trunk mode and created R1 sub-interfaces Gi0/0.10, Gi0/0.20, and Gi0/0.30 with dot1Q tagging.

  3. Apply DHCP, NAT/PAT & ACL Policies

    Configured per-VLAN IP pools, outbound NAT overload on R1 Gi0/1, and explicit SSH administrative access restricted to IT VLAN 20.

  4. Validate Connectivity & Broadcast Isolation

    Verified cross-VLAN pings, traceroute hops through R1, and confirmed broadcast containment within each /26 subnet.
