About me

I am pursuing a B.Sc. in Computer Science and Engineering at Daffodil International University, where computer networking quickly became the discipline I enjoy most. Through structured training in CCNA and MTCNA at Creative IT Institute, I built a strong foundation in IPv4 subnetting, routing, switching, VLAN segmentation, and router administration.

I prefer hands-on lab work over passive slides—most of my understanding comes from designing multi-department topologies, breaking configurations intentionally, and diagnosing them step by step. I am now seeking an entry-level Network Engineer role or internship where I can work on production infrastructure and continue growing.
